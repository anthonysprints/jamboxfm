CONNECT & INSTALL

Bring your FM-1 along.

Turn on your FM-1 and connect it with a USB data cable. Connect only one FM-1. Close other MIDI apps and browser instrument tabs, then connect below.

Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Allow MIDI and system exclusive access when your browser asks.

You can check a selected device here. To install, disconnect the other matching MIDI devices so only one FM-1 remains.

Waiting for your FM-1

Checking the firmware download…

This experimental install replaces your FM-1’s firmware. An interruption can require hardware recovery. Keep your factory firmware and recovery backup saved on your computer.
