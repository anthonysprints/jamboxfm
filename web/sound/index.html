Why did some controls disappear?
Different engines use different sound-building methods. This test hides modulation routes they do not use, while keeping the stored settings. For example, Analog shape modulation needs a PWM wave, while the organ uses its own tone controls. The working FX track filter remains available.

      
What stays in the normal installer?
The complete tested drum workflow, including individual synthesized-drum editing, and intentional manual saving. This separate trial adds the new melodic sound-page cleanup. Your existing recovery files and Save now workflow still apply.
