AFTER THE INSTRUMENT RETURNS

      
Try the sound of your beat.

      
    
        
  1. Check your saved jam. Start at low volume. Confirm your existing loop, tempo and edited sounds returned. Do not reinitialize saving.
    2. 
        
  2. Shape the whole kit. Select Drums and tap EDIT for KIT SOUND. Play the pads. The four right knobs choose Kit, Level, Tone and Pan. Try different kits; your beat should stay the same.
    3. 
        
  3. Add effects. Tap FX for KIT FX: Filter, Echo, Room and Pan affect the whole drum part. Repeated FX taps reach SLICER, INSERT and INSERT 2, then ALL ECHO and ALL ROOM. Those last two change shared effects across the jam.
    4. 
        
  4. Keep the beat editor close. SEQ opens GRID, with Drum, Step, Strength and Repeat on the right knobs. White keys edit steps. SEQ keeps GRID open; EDIT always returns to KIT SOUND.
    5. 
        
  5. Check familiar controls. In GRID, KIT SOUND and drum FX pages, SELECT changes BPM, PRESETS chooses a curated kit, and ALGORITHM switches parts in Home's order. Held shortcuts keep their usual controls.
    6. 
        
  6. Try the helpful taps. ENV points you to EDIT, LFO points you to FX, SEL explains drum pads, and ARP reminds you to hold ARP with a pad for rolls. These taps should keep your current workspace.
    7. 
        
  7. Jam as usual. Check held SEQ, held EDIT erase/undo, live FX and rolls. HOME remembers GROOVE or SOUND; REC from GRID returns Home to playable pads. Check recording, sounding lights, and the other parts' sound and FX pages too.

Drum grid controls
Drum / Step / Strength / Repeat are the four right knobs. Strength goes from ghost to hard; Repeat gives 1–4 hits within a step. Empty cells stay empty until you add a hit. The first four black keys select step pages. The grid stays on your editing page while playback continues.

      
Kit sound and shared effects
KIT SOUND changes the whole kit. Its Kit knob offers the broader usable kit list; PRESETS keeps the eight curated choices. Tone and the KIT FX Filter are the same filter, with low-pass and high-pass directions. Echo and Room set how much of the drum part enters each effect. ALL ECHO and ALL ROOM change the shared effects themselves, so other parts using them can change too. Individual drum sound editing comes later.
