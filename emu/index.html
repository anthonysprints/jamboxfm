YOUR FIRST MINUTE

Find the pocket.

  1. 01

    Get it moving

    Start audio, then press PLAY on the instrument. The Dusty session is ready.

  2. 02

    Pick your part

    Turn ALGORITHM for drums, bass, chords or lead. Try the keys. Turn PRESETS for another sound, and SELECT to change the BPM.

  3. 03

    Make it yours

    GROOVE shows the four patterns. Tap HOME for the larger SOUND controls; tap again to switch back. In GROOVE, KNOB 1–4 adjust swing, level, steps and pan; SOUND provides the sound-shaping controls.

Playing with your computer keyboard +

The instrument starts on GROOVE Home. Tap HOME to switch between GROOVE and SOUND while your music keeps playing. From FX, ENV, EDIT or another page, HOME returns to your last Home view. The left knobs and playable keys keep their roles. GROOVE knobs 1–4 control shared SWING and the selected part’s LEVEL / STEPS / PAN; SOUND knobs 1–4 shape its sound. Changing Level preserves mute; shortening Steps keeps hidden notes for when you extend the loop again. Hold HOME for settings; a short HOME tap closes settings without switching views.

GROOVE shows 16 stored steps per part and follows each part's playback position. Each row labels its visible range and total length; on stop it keeps the last range shown. These are pattern steps, not necessarily whole bars. Hold SEQ to edit, or hold SEQ and tap HOME to keep that editing layer open. A quick SEQ tap opens its remembered sequencer page.

POLY recording remembers each note’s own length. Hold one note, add another later and release them separately; the loop keeps that overlap. Starts and endings snap to the step grid. For individual notes on Bass or Lead, choose the sound first, tap EDIT, turn SELECT to VOICE and set knob 1 to POLY, with CHORD OFF. Bass still starts in its one-note LEGATO mode. Turning CHORD on already enables POLY.

Chords and Lead start with SNAP: every key plays, including black keys. Lights show the keys you press plus the notes playing on the selected part, including all sounding chord tones. For a single note corrected by SNAP, both the pressed key and the corrected note light up. Tap SEL; if SEL 2 opens, turn SELECT one click left to reach the first page. Knob 1 chooses the scale root, knob 2 the scale, knob 3 QNT, and knob 4 CHORD. Tap HOME to return.

Choose MAJOR, MINOR or DIM to build that chord shape from the key you play. TRIAD, 7TH and 9TH build chords from the selected scale. CHORD OFF plays single notes. With QNT OFF, each printed key is the root; SNAP corrects only the root. WHITE is the alternate layout where white keys choose scale degrees and black keys modify held chords.

Turning chords on allows the full chord to sound on Bass or Lead too. For inversions, tap SEL and use SELECT to reach SEL 2. Knob 3 changes INVERT: ROOT keeps the original order, AUTO finds a smooth voicing, and +1 / +2 / +3 move the lowest note up an octave that many times. Knob 2 adds STRUM.

The lower keys use Z–/ with black notes on S D F H J L ; '. The upper keys use Q W E R T Y U I O. The printed shortcuts on the panel show each note.

The number row controls the twelve buttons. [ and ] shift octaves; Space presses PLAY; Esc releases all notes. Focused knobs respond to the arrow keys. Hold a function key to use its second layer.
