YOUR FIRST MINUTE

Find the pocket.

  1. 01

    Get it moving

    Start audio, then press PLAY on the instrument. The Dusty session is ready.

  2. 02

    Pick your part

    Turn SELECT for drums, bass, chords or lead. Try the keys. Turn PRESETS for another sound.

  3. 03

    Make it yours

    Shape the sound with KNOB 1–4. Tap FX, ENV or EDIT to explore. HOME brings you back.

Playing with your computer keyboard +

Lead starts with SNAP: every key plays in the chosen scale, and neighboring keys may share a note. Tap SEL and set QNT to OFF for normal keyboard notes. In Chords, hold a white key and use black keys to change the chord.

The lower keys use Z–/ with black notes on S D F H J L ; '. The upper keys use Q W E R T Y U I O. The printed shortcuts on the panel show each note.

The number row controls the twelve buttons. [ and ] shift octaves; Space presses PLAY; Esc releases all notes. Focused knobs respond to the arrow keys. Hold a function key to use its second layer.
